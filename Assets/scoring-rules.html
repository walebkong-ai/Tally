Your game. Two ways to score.

Solvry scoring v1 · Published November 11, 2026. Select Placement points or Performance points on the overall leaderboard. Your original game results remain unchanged. Results are self-reported; Solvry does not verify publisher accounts or difficulty.

Placement points

Daily places earn 10, 7, 5, 3, 2, then 1 point. Lower guesses, moves, mistakes, par or time win; higher game points win. Completion-only solves tie. Ties share a place and points, then skip places: 1st, 1st, 3rd. Placement depends on scores available in your own friends group.

Performance points

Eligible results earn 0–100 points per game, independent of opponents. These are Solvry formulas and benchmarks, not official publisher ratings or claims of equal puzzle difficulty. Each game’s points are rounded to the nearest whole number, clamped to 0–100, then summed for the selected day or last seven days. Playing more games can increase your total.

  • Missed, malformed or invalid results earn no points. Unfinished guess, time, strike, par and completion puzzles remain unranked.
  • For scored point/depth games, a valid saved daily total can count even when marked Played. A valid zero-point performance may rank; an unplayed account does not.
  • RNGdle and custom games have their own boards and earn no combined points in either mode. Color earns combined points only from a matching dated Color Daily share.
  • Use the same publisher, daily date, language and mode. Follow each game’s assistance guidance below. No automatic import support is implied.
  • Changing scoring views recalculates derived points without resaving, modifying or deleting original results.
